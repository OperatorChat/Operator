OptionsNew chat
Slate, darkThe same layout in the dark variant: charcoal paper, a lighter blue for the selected row so it stays legible. All four themes and all three text sizes carry over because the redesign only touches layouts and theme attributes.

What actually changes

  1. Kill the boxes. Rows, bubbles and fields lose their outlines (outline width becomes 0 in the three normal themes; High contrast keeps its heavy strokes). Separation comes from spacing and a hairline.
  2. One surface. The status bar and Android navigation bar are painted the theme background with dark (or light) icons, and the soft‑key bar becomes a hairline‑topped strip in the same colour with accent‑coloured labels. This is one of the biggest wins and costs almost nothing.
  3. A real typeface. Bundle Inter (open licence, ~300 KB) with weights 400/600/700. Roboto on a 120 dpi panel is what makes it feel like an old Android; Inter at 12–16 sp stays crisp and has a proper semibold.
  4. Avatars. A coloured circle with one or two initials, colour picked from a fixed palette by hashing the chat name, so it's stable. Room avatars from Matrix can replace it later; nothing in the data layer changes.
  5. Hierarchy in the list. Name semibold, preview and time in the secondary colour, unread as bold + count pill. The header shows the screen's name and a small summary rather than repeating the focused row.
  6. Bubbles. Mine in the accent with white text, theirs in soft grey. Delivery state is shown by ticks, with only two colour exceptions: a paler tint while sending and a red tint on failure. The focused message gets a 2 dp accent ring, so it isn't recoloured.
  7. Settings as grouped lists with a small coloured glyph and the current value as a subtitle.
  8. Options as a sheet that rises over the dimmed screen, instead of a full screen of boxes.
  9. Motion, lightly. Focus moves with a 120 ms colour fade, screens slide in from the right and back out, the sheet slides up. All standard View animations, cheap enough for the test phone's chip; a setting can turn them off.
  10. Nothing functional moves. Same key map, same soft‑key labels, same menus and items, same themes and text sizes. It's layouts, drawables, colours and one font.

Rough effort: the surface, boxes, typeface and avatars are a day; bubbles, settings groups and the sheet are another; motion and a pass over the onboarding screens a third. It can land as small commits with the test phone checked after each.
