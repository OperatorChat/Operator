Operator is a chat app for keypad phones: Android feature phones with a D-pad, soft keys and a T9 keypad. It shows WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, Messenger, Instagram, Discord and more in one list, with notifications that work without Google services, on phones that would struggle with any ordinary chat app.

It is a Matrix client signed in to a Beeper account. Beeper connects your chat networks and places the messages in an encrypted Matrix account; Operator is the keypad-phone window onto that account. You need a Beeper account with your chat apps connected, set up on a computer with Beeper Desktop.

What it does
• All your chats in one list, driven entirely by the keypad. The screen always says what the two soft keys do.
• Read and reply, with delivery and read marks, replies, edits, delete for everyone, and reactions.
• Photos on demand: nothing downloads until you ask. Send photos from the gallery or camera. Open or save files.
• Mentions: see when you've been named, mute a group except for mentions, mention people from the member list.
• Notifications in seconds through a tiny relay that never sees message content. Optional screen wake.
• Four looks, light and dark, three text sizes. Turn-to-preview on phones that rotate.

Privacy
Messages are end-to-end encrypted by Matrix. Operator's database on the phone is encrypted with a key in the phone's hardware keystore. The notification relay receives only a content-free wake-up signal. No analytics, no crash reporting, no Google services, no servers of our own.

Beta
Operator is in public beta and in daily use on the TTfone TT990 and other keypad phones. Settings → Report a problem drafts an email with the technical details (never your messages).

Operator is an free-software project (GPL-3.0), not affiliated with Beeper, Matrix.org or any phone maker. It depends on Beeper's service. Donations (ko-fi.com/operatorchat) pay for a dedicated notification relay.
